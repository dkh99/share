The proposed mobile order

  1. Choose workspace, find a task and filter by relevant status.
  2. Open the task and its material shelf.
  3. Read PDF or live documents; watch MP4 and listen to audio.
  4. Choose the associated agent and continue in its real terminal.
  5. Handle questions, hidden selections, offline states and errors.
  6. Review Rituals, code changes, history and notifications.
  7. Review the result; you decide when the work is done.
